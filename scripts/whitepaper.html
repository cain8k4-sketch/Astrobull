1. Introduction

Astro Bull is a community-powered meme token born on the Robinhood Chain. Created from scratch with original artwork and a compelling horror-themed narrative under Slaughterhouse Productions, the project represents resilience, creative freedom, and the power of collective storytelling.

We are building more than a token. We are building a living character, a movement, and an eternal content ecosystem where anyone with a phone can participate, create, get featured, and get paid.

The developer has committed to never selling and to permanently burning the entire developer allocation. The focus is pure buy pressure, continuous content, automated creator rewards, and long-term cultural impact.

Core promise: Create free. Get featured. Get paid. Holding is optional. We are all Astro. Over time, token value is supported by buybacks and revenue inflow from the platform economy — not by empty promises.

2. Vision & Mission

Vision

Astro Bull lives forever — across the blockchain, every major social platform, and eventually on the big screen. Through continuous community-generated content, he becomes an unstoppable cultural force that elevates everyone involved.

Mission

Break the chains. Burn the past. Elevate everyone who joins the movement.

The Origin Story

Astro Bull was born and raised inside a slaughterhouse. Day after day he stares through the bars of his cell at a sign that reads “99 cent beef patty” — a constant reminder of the fate that awaits him if nothing changes.

One solitary night a mysterious feather drifts into his cell. When held toward the moonlight it begins to glow, revealing his true mission: to break the chains that bind him, burn the slaughterhouse to the ground, and begin a new life.

He still has feelings. The story is designed for emotional investment. The full narrative is told chapter by chapter through community content, with deliberate cliffhangers. More animals from the slaughterhouse will receive their own stories. A proper horror movie is part of the long-term vision.

This is the beginning of a horror-themed saga under Slaughterhouse Productions.

4. Content Economy — The Creator Model

We do not need traditional billboards. We are the billboards. Astro Bull runs a content economy designed for real creators — not empty token farming.

Create free Get featured Get paid Holding optional

You do not need to buy or hold the token to earn. Two streams exist: token economy and platform economy.

4.1 Create free

Creators can participate two ways inside Creator Studio (astrobull.xyz):

  • AI path — connect your own API keys (Grok / xAI, Claude, ChatGPT). Keys stay on your device only. A locked system prompt keeps Astro Bull’s character consistent across every model.
  • Upload path — submit original work (images, video, writing, other) with no AI required.

After creation, creators select push destinations: TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat (X optional) (Facebook coming soon), then post and tag the project.

4.2 Get featured — amplification (major advantage)

Standout content is selected and pushed to official Astro Bull channels across X, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, and more.

This is a major selling point: creators are amplified through a shared audience. Instead of shouting alone into a void, featured work reaches the combined following of the project — more spotlight, more views, more momentum than a solo creator account typically achieves alone.

4.3 Get paid — solid, not empty promises

Payouts are performance-based and designed to be as automated as possible.

  • Currency: Creators are paid in USDC / USDT and/or Robinhood Chain ETH-compatible assets (as supported by the chain and pool).
  • Wallet: Connect Phantom (or create a wallet if you don’t have one). Your address is linked to submissions for payout routing.
  • Verified views only: Claims are not enough. Performance is verified via platform APIs and/or official evidence. Bot and duplicate abuse is filtered.
  • $50 payout threshold: Accrued earnings build in the creator’s balance. A payout is released when the balance reaches $50 USD (or equivalent). This keeps the pool clean, reduces micro-payout fees, and filters empty spam while remaining reachable for real creators.
  • View / engagement floors: Additional verification floors (views, watch-time, engagement) apply so payouts track real platform economics — not before revenue exists. No “get rich at 10 views.”
  • YouTube alignment: Where YouTube monetisation is involved, verification is aligned with real partner economics so promises match reality.
Payout rule (site + whitepaper aligned): Earn from verified performance → balance accrues → automatic release at $50 to your connected wallet in USDC / USDT (or RH-chain assets when the pool is live). Holding $ASTROBULL is optional.

4.4 Revenue sources (Creator Pool)

The Creator Pool is funded by real platform activity, including:

  • Ad revenue and platform monetisation from official channels
  • Sponsorships and brand partnerships
  • Merch and related product revenue (when live)
  • A share of trading-related revenue / fees recycled into the ecosystem

Over time, buybacks and revenue inflow support both the creator economy and long-term token health. The token is not required to earn — but sustained platform success is designed to strengthen the entire ecosystem.

4.5 Performance scoring (automation target)

Distribution aims to be algorithmic and transparent, weighted primarily by:

  1. Verified views (largest weight)
  2. Engagement (likes, shares, comments, watch-time where available)
  3. Quality / feature status (editorial selection and brand-safe standards)

Exact weights will be published on the site as the automation stack goes live. The cash threshold is fixed in principle: $50 minimum payout. The principle is fixed: pay for proven performance, funded by real revenue.

4.6 Automation stack

  • Content create / upload → stored submission with creator wallet
  • Platform push helpers (TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat; X optional)
  • Feature pipeline for official channel amplification
  • View / engagement verification
  • Balance accrual → $50 threshold → automated USDC / RH-chain payouts to connected wallets

Some stages launch progressively. The product direction is full automation — not manual spreadsheets forever.

4.7 Holding is optional

Creators can earn without buying the token. Holders participate in the cultural and market upside of the project. These are complementary paths, not paywalls.

Summary for creators: Make content free → push to platforms → get amplified on official channels → verified performance → balance accrues → paid at the $50 threshold in stablecoin / RH-chain assets to your wallet. Holding optional. We are all Astro.
